System Analysis

System Title

Smart Faculty Queue & Appointment System

Problem Statement

Faculty consultations at the school are handled by walk-in lines and ad-hoc messages: students wait without knowing their position, faculty double-book themselves, and no record of consultations is kept.

Objectives

  1. Give faculty one online place to publish consultation availability.
  2. Let students book appointments and join walk-in queues without schedule conflicts.
  3. Show students their live queue position and estimated waiting time.
  4. Give faculty tools to run consultation sessions and record notes.
  5. Keep a complete history and audit trail, with reports and user management for administrators.

Users / Actors

  • Student – searches faculty, books appointments, joins queues, views history.
  • Faculty – manages availability and queue, serves students, records consultation notes.
  • Administrator – manages user accounts, views reports and audit logs.

Scope

A web application with three roles and six modules: Authentication, Availability, Booking and Queue, Session Workflow, Analytics and History, User Management and Audit. Out of scope: email/SMS notifications, payments, mobile app.

Functional requirements

  1. FR1 Users register/log in with role-based access.
  2. FR2 Faculty create/remove availability slots.
  3. FR3 Students search, filter and sort faculty.
  4. FR4 Students book an appointment in a free slot.
  5. FR5 Students join a faculty's walk-in queue and receive a ticket number.
  6. FR6 Students see live position and estimated wait.
  7. FR7 Faculty open/close queue, call next, complete or mark no-show.
  8. FR8 Faculty record consultation notes (logs).
  9. FR9 Students/faculty cancel appointments or leave queue.
  10. FR10 Admin manages users (create, edit, deactivate, reset password).
  11. FR11 Dashboards and reports per role; sortable history.
  12. FR12 Admin audit log of key actions.

Business rules

  1. BR1 A slot can be booked by only one student.
  2. BR2 A student cannot hold two appointments at the same date/time.
  3. BR3 A student may have at most 3 active appointments.
  4. BR4 A student may hold only one active ticket per faculty; queue must be open.
  5. BR5 Only one student is served at a time per faculty; slots with booking history cannot be deleted.
  6. BR6 Slots cannot be added or booked for past dates.

Advanced feature

Live queue position and estimated wait time (10 min × position, refreshed automatically every 10 s), plus open/close queue control.

Associative entities: students and faculty are both rows of USERS, so the many-to-many "student consults faculty" is resolved by two associative entities: APPOINTMENTS (scheduled, linked to a FACULTY_SCHEDULES slot) and QUEUE_TICKETS (walk-in). Each carries two foreign keys to USERS (student_id, faculty_id). A slot may have many appointment rows over time (cancelled, then rebooked) but only one booked/completed row at once (partial unique index). AUDIT_LOGS keeps the acting username as text and has no foreign key.

Database transactions: book appointment, join queue, call next, finish ticket (status + log) and complete appointment each run inside one BEGIN/COMMIT/ROLLBACK transaction. Unique indexes also enforce BR1, BR4, one serving ticket per faculty, and unique ticket numbers.
